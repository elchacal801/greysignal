This trend indicates an increasing financial and reputational risk for companies failing to adhere to stringent data protection requirements.

The ongoing geopolitical tensions, exacerbated by cyber-attacks targeting critical infrastructure, could lead to increased volatility in global financial markets. Notably, the energy sector in Europe and telecommunications in the U.S. are at heightened risk due to potential disruptions. Furthermore, regulatory actions in the EU and UK to enhance oversight of critical third parties may introduce compliance challenges for multinational companies, impacting their operational costs and strategic planning.
